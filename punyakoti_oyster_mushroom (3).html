Nutrition & wellness

What do you get from eating oyster mushrooms?

Oyster mushrooms can be a useful part of a varied, balanced diet, providing nutrients and naturally occurring compounds.

💪 Protein

Provides plant-based protein that can contribute to daily protein intake as part of meals.

🌾 Dietary fibre

Contains dietary fibre, an important part of a balanced eating pattern.

⚡ B vitamins

Provides B-group vitamins involved in normal energy metabolism and other body functions.

🪨 Minerals

Contains minerals such as potassium and phosphorus, adding to nutrient variety.

🌿 Beta-glucans

Contains naturally occurring beta-glucans, a group of polysaccharides found in mushrooms.

🛡️ Antioxidant compounds

Mushrooms contain naturally occurring compounds with antioxidant activity.

🥗 Light & satisfying

Their texture makes them a satisfying addition to many lighter dishes.

🍽️ Everyday versatility

Easy to include across breakfast, lunch and dinner for more meal variety.

Good food, not a medicine: Oyster mushrooms provide useful nutrients, but they should be enjoyed as part of a varied diet and are not a treatment or cure for disease.
